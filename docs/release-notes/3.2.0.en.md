Paneldeck 3.2.0 adds a **Time tab** with alarms, timers, a calendar with recurring events and iCal import/export, stopwatches, world clocks and birthdays, and turns the editor into a more complete tool: a **menu bar** in every split, **JSON/XML formatting** (only when you ask, or on save through a rule), a **diff tab** with change markers and **horizontal scrolling**. Sessions get their own **environment variables**, agent access can now be limited **per project**, themes can have their own **background**, and paths follow the target shell everywhere, including **WSL** in the editor. Approval prompts became harder to confirm without looking, and several security fixes keep agents out of Paneldeck's data folder and away from projects they may not see. Settings, chats, sessions and the agent interface stay compatible.

> Independent open-source project – not affiliated with, endorsed by or reviewed by Anthropic.
> The installer and `paneldeck.exe` are **not code-signed** yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first start ("More info" → "Run anyway"). The macOS version is experimental and not signed or notarized. See the README for details.

### Added

- **Time tab** at the end of the title bar (**Alt+Shift+4**, also in a new window with Ctrl+click) with the sub-tabs Alarms, Timers, Calendar, Stopwatches, Clocks and Birthdays – each a list or view with a detail pane on the right (#164).
- **Alarms:** time plus weekdays or once, name, built-in or own WAV/MP3 sound with preview, snooze (5 min, at most 10 times, changeable per alarm). They ring while Paneldeck runs, also minimized or in the tray; missed alarms (Paneldeck closed, computer asleep) are shown as a hint at the next start ("Missed: alarm 07:00 (Mon)"), also a snooze that ended meanwhile; after the system clock is set back alarms count from the new time. Daylight saving time: a time in the gap rings at the next valid time, the repeated hour rings once (#164).
- **Timers:** several named countdowns at the same time with pause, resume, restart and reset, and saved presets ("Tea 4 min"); they survive restarts and sleep – a timer that ran out meanwhile shows "expired at 14:32" (#164).
- **Calendar:** month, week and day views and a list with details on the right; all-day and multi-day events as bars at the top. Own events with title, time, duration, note, colour, reminder and recurrence. Due dates of tickets and to-do cards, sprints, versions/milestones and booked time can be shown – each switchable in *Options …* for all projects and per project (due dates are on at first open) (#164).
- **Recurring events:** a builder (daily, weekdays, weekly on chosen days, monthly on a day or the n-th weekday, yearly, every X minutes/hours) produces a cron expression that experts can edit directly (5 fields, `@daily` …, `MON#1` for the n-th weekday), with end date or count, single-day exceptions, a preview of the next 5 occurrences and a warning about cron's day-of-month OR day-of-week case. Times are local: a time in the daylight-saving gap moves to the next valid time, the repeated hour fires once (#164).
- **iCal import and export** of local `.ics` files: recurrences are translated to and from `RRULE` as far as possible, the export includes the time zone; the import skips duplicates by UID and broken entries one by one, shows a report and accepts files up to 10 MB (#164).
- **Stopwatches** with laps (start, stop, lap, reset, lap list) that keep running across restarts (#164).
- **World clocks:** city or time zone (IANA database), time, date and offset to local time, digital or analog (#164).
- **Birthdays:** name and date with an optional year; age and countdown such as "37 in 5 days and 2 months", "Today! 🎂" on the day, 29 February shown on 28 February in non-leap years, a reminder per entry (off by default) (#164).
- **Notifications of the Time tab:** sound and toast for alarms and timer ends (on); event reminders of the calendar are off by default (calendar options) and, when switched on, notify the same way – missed ones are shown at the next start; taskbar flashing (off), volume and ring duration – all in *Options* of the Time tab (`settings.json` → `time`); five built-in sounds generated by Paneldeck itself (#164).
- **Time tracking from timers and stopwatches** (off by default): with a ticket set, stopping asks "Book 47 min on PAN-12?" and adds a time entry to the ticket (#164).
- **Tutorial:** new chapter "Time" (tab, sub-tabs, a new alarm, timer presets, calendar views, notification options); its steps also appear in "What's new" (#164).
- **Agent interface:** `list_alarms`, `list_timers`, `list_stopwatches`, `list_clocks`, `list_birthdays`, `list_calendar_events` (Read) and `create_alarm`, `update_alarm`, `create_timer`, `update_timer`, `create_stopwatch`, `control_stopwatch`, `create_calendar_event`, `update_calendar_event` (Control); the list tools are paged (`limit`/`offset`), nothing can be deleted, clocks and birthdays are read-only (#164).
- **Menu bar in every editor split:** *File · Edit · View · Format* above the tab bar, like Notepad++ or Sublime Text – New, Open, Save, Save as, Save all, Close tab; Undo, Redo, clipboard, Find/Replace, Find in files, Go to line and the multi-cursor and line commands; Word wrap, Minimap, Folder tree, Split right/down, Text/Syntax mode and language. Shortcuts are shown on the right, entries that do not apply are greyed out and say why on hover (e.g. *Not JSON or XML*), and in narrow splits the menus fold into one *Menu ▾*. It can be switched off under *Settings → Editor → Menu bar* (on by default). The command palette (Ctrl+Shift+P) now also opens in the Editor tab (#381).
- **Formatting JSON and XML in the editor:** the *Format* menu formats (Alt+Shift+F), minifies (Alt+Shift+M), validates (Alt+Shift+V) and sorts JSON keys – offline, with the indentation from `.editorconfig` or the file, applied directly as **one undo step**, on the selection only if there is one. Invalid content is never changed: a bar shows the error with line and column and a *Go to* button; *Validate* shows "Valid JSON/XML". Formatting runs in the background with a progress line and *Cancel*, so the editor stays responsive – also for large files from 20 MB. Minifying JSONC asks first because it removes the comments. JSON and XML are recognised by extension and well-known names (content only for untitled tabs and files without an extension), and a language chosen in the status bar wins. The shortcuts can be changed under *Settings → Shortcuts* (they avoid Ctrl+Alt, which is AltGr on many keyboards; Ctrl+Shift+I is a second binding for formatting in case Alt+Shift switches the input language), and the commands are in the command palette as *Format: …* (#381).
- **Formatting only when you ask:** nothing is ever formatted automatically. It happens through the *Format* menu, the shortcuts or the command palette – or on save through an *action on save* rule with the *Format (JSON/XML)* action. *Format → Format on save …* opens the rule dialog for the current file with the pattern (e.g. `*.json`) and the action already filled in. Without such a rule, saving writes exactly the text in the editor – also on autosave and when an agent saves. There is no separate setting for it (#381, #383).
- **MCP tool `format_file`** for documents open in the editor: `op` = `validate` (level Read, no switch), `format`, `minify` or `sort_keys` (level Control plus the new switch *Agents may format* in *Settings → Editor → Agents*, off by default). The change stays unsaved, is marked as made by the agent and can be undone; agents never take the keyboard focus. Chats with local models use the same rules while a document is open (the tool is off by default for chats and checked per model under Settings → Models); in plan mode only `validate` runs (#381).
- **JSON and JSONC formatting in the editor:** *Format* pretty-prints with the file's indentation (spaces or tabs), *Minify* removes all whitespace, *Sort JSON keys* sorts object keys recursively (stable, by Unicode code point; array order stays) and formats the result, *Validate* reports the first error with line, column and a clear message (e.g. "expected ',' or '}' after object member") and changes nothing. Values are kept exactly as written: numbers are never rewritten (`1.0`, `1e400`, `0.1000` stay), string escapes stay (`é`), key order and duplicate keys are kept, empty objects and arrays become `{}` / `[]`, one blank line between members is kept, a byte order mark stays. **JSONC** (`.jsonc`, `tsconfig.json`, `.vscode/*.json` or files with comments): `//` and `/* */` comments and trailing commas are kept – a comment on its own line stays on its own line, a comment at the end of a line stays there and moves with its member when sorting, a block comment in front of a member on the same line (`/* about a */ "a": 2`) belongs to that member; *Minify* drops comments and trailing commas. In plain JSON, comments and trailing commas are reported as errors with their position. Works offline; 50 MB files take a few seconds, deeply nested files (10 000 levels) work, indentation stops growing after 100 levels (#381).
- **XML formatting in the editor:** *Format* indents elements gently – comments, CDATA sections, processing instructions, the XML declaration and the DOCTYPE (with internal subset) stay exactly as written, as do elements with mixed content (text next to tags, e.g. XHTML paragraphs), text-only elements, whitespace-only content (e.g. `<xsl:text> </xsl:text>`), inline siblings separated only by spaces (`<b>a</b> <i>b</i>`, in documents with mixed content also `<b>a</b><i>b</i>`) and `xml:space="preserve"` subtrees; tags stay as written (attributes, order, quoting, spacing; continuation lines of a multi-line start tag follow the new indentation of its first line), entity and character references are kept as written, `<a/>` and `<a></a>` keep their form, one blank line between elements is kept, beyond 100 levels the indentation stops growing. *Minify* removes the whitespace between elements and keeps comments. *Validate* checks well-formedness (matching tags, attributes, a single root element, names, unescaped `<` and `&`) and reports line and column; nothing is changed. Works offline: external DTDs and entities are never loaded; large files (50 MB) take about a second (#381).
- Editor: **actions on save** (#383) – rules per file, folder (recursive) or pattern (`.gitignore` syntax, e.g. `src/**/*.ts`, `!dist/**`) in *Settings → Editor → Actions on save*. Before writing: trim trailing whitespace, final newline, line endings, encoding, tabs ↔ spaces, format JSON/XML (the only way a file is formatted on save – nothing is ever formatted automatically; *Format → Format on save …* opens the rule dialog for the current file with the pattern and the action filled in); afterwards in order: run a command (hidden like the process list or visible in a terminal, variables such as `${file}`/`${relativeFile}` quoted for the shell – a variable inside a quoted string is refused –, also as `PANELDECK_FILE` … in the environment, own encrypted environment variables, 60 s time limit with *Cancel*), copy the file to another local folder, send a command to a session waiting at its prompt, restart a process or group, show a notification. The file is always saved; errors show a red dot on the tab, a status bar entry and a notice with *Show output* (last 100 runs, optional rotating log). Autosave and agent saves only trigger when a rule allows it; *Save without actions*, *Pause* until restart and a master switch; *Test for file …*, *Run now*, *Rules for this file* in the folder tree; export/import without secret values (imported rules arrive switched off). Paneldeck itself never accesses the network – uploads only through your own commands. Stored in the new data file `save-actions.json`.
- Agent interface: new tool `list_save_actions` (level Read; names, patterns, action kinds, on/off and last result – never commands or values); `editor_save` reports which rules ran or were skipped (`save_actions`) (#383).
- Editor: file comparison as its own tab (*a.json ↔ b.json*), oriented on Sublime Text/Sublime Merge – compare a file with its saved version, git HEAD, the git index, any revision (HEAD, index, branches, tags, the last 50 commits of the file or a typed revision, checked first), the clipboard or another file; from the tab context menu (*Compare with …*), the folder tree (*Select for compare* / *Compare with …*, or Ctrl+click two files) and the command palette, which now also opens in the Editor tab. Side by side with synchronised scrolling and hatched filler lines (unified automatically below ~700 px, switchable), changes within lines word by word, syntax colours and line numbers on both sides, unchanged regions collapsed with 3 context lines (expandable), block arrows in both directions (one undo step each, in the same document as the normal tab), *Swap sides*, F7/Shift+F7 for the next/previous change (configurable), *Ignore whitespace*, an info bar for differences in line endings, encoding/BOM and the final newline, *Copy diff*, *Copy block*, *Save as patch …* (git-compatible). Updates live while typing; large files are compared in the background with progress and *Cancel*, binary files show size and SHA-256. Comparison tabs are restored after a restart. Everything offline – only the local git (#385)
- Editor: change markers in the gutter (*Settings → Editor*, off by default): added, modified and removed lines against the git index and HEAD at the same time – unstaged and staged changes in their own colours – or against the saved file outside a repository; also in the scrollbar and the minimap. A click on a marker shows the old lines with *Revert* (one undo step), previous/next change and *Open in diff* (#385)
- Themes: new colours for the comparison and the change markers (`diff_add_bg`, `diff_remove_bg`, `diff_add_word`, `diff_remove_word`, `diff_filler`, `marker_*`), with contrast checks, in the theme editor group *Editor: comparison and change markers* (#385)
- Agent interface: new tool `editor_diff` (level Read): unified diff and changed blocks of an open document against its saved file, git HEAD/index or another open document; marked as untrusted, size-limited; for external agents like `editor_read` (#385)
- Terminals: **environment variables per session** – new tab *Environment* in *New session*, in the session settings and in the template editor: a table with *Name*, *Value*, *Secret*, *Mode* (*Replace*, *Prepend*, *Append* with the platform separator – for `PATH` and other lists), *Remove* (removes an inherited variable) and, for WSL, *Path* (convert via `WSLENV /p`, lists `/l`); up to 100 variables, *Paste KEY=VALUE …* and a one-time *Import from .env …* with a preview and a checkbox per line. **Secret** values are stored encrypted like the process list, masked, never typed, copied, exported or given to agents; the flag is preset for names containing TOKEN, KEY, SECRET, PASS, PWD or AUTH. Order: profile < template < session; values are literal (no `%VAR%`/`$VAR`); WSL sessions get all variables through `WSLENV`. Names used by shell integration (`PROMPT`, `PROMPT_COMMAND`, `PS0`, `ZDOTDIR`, `WSLENV`) and by Claude Code (`ANTHROPIC_*`, `CLAUDE_CODE_*`) get a warning, and Claude sessions note that Claude and its tools see all values. New optional fields `env` per session in `state.json` and `templates.json` (#384)
- Terminals: after changing the variables of a running session Paneldeck asks **Restart / Set now / Later**; *Set now* clears the input line and then types the normal values into the shell as a visible command with a leading space (PowerShell `$env:`, cmd `set`, bash/zsh `export`, fish `set -gx`, also in WSL) – only when the shell has shell integration and waits at its prompt. The pane header shows `$ n` with a tooltip of the names (secret ones as ••••) and an orange dot while a change only applies after a restart. Split and *Duplicate* copy the variables; the sidebar context menu has *Environment variables → Edit …, Copy variables, Paste variables* (secret values never reach the clipboard) and *Copy as .env* (secret values empty, prepend/append as a `# paneldeck: …` comment line that paste and import read back). *Save as terminal template …* takes secret values only when you tick it (#384)
- Terminals: a session whose secret variables cannot be decrypted at start waits (*Secret variables locked – unlock and start*, like administrator sessions); if the values cannot be decrypted at all (other user or computer), *Edit variables …* opens the tab to enter them again (#384)
- Agent interface: `list_sessions`, `whoami` and `create_session` report the session's variables in a new field `env` – names and flags (`secret`, `set_by_agent`, optional `remove`/`mode`), **never values**. New switch *Settings → Agents → Agents may set environment variables* (off by default, new optional `settings.json` fields `mcp_session_env` and `mcp_env_allow`): then `create_session` takes `env` for the session it creates – at most 20 variables, values up to 1 KB without control characters, never secret; path, startup, loader, proxy, Git, `ANTHROPIC_*`, `CLAUDE_*` and `PANELDECK_*` variables are always refused, and you can limit the names to patterns such as `APP_*`; violations return `bad_args`, `env` without the switch `forbidden`. Agent names must be plain ASCII identifiers. Template variables an agent may not set and all secret template variables are left out when an agent starts a template (`template_ignored`: `env:NAME`); the call log contains only the names (#384)
- Agent access per project (#386): every sidebar project, every ticket project and the new area “General” (everything without a project) gets its own agent level – As global, Off, Read only or Control – plus the switch “For all”. An agent running in a Paneldeck session works in that session's project; other projects only when they are released “For all”, external agents only “For all” projects. The global level stays the upper bound. Settings → Agents shows a table of all projects; projects also have “Agent access …” in their context menu and in the ticket project settings, and locked projects show a lock in the project bar. Ticket projects can be linked to a sidebar project and then follow its level.
- Existing projects, the system project TODO and “General” keep “As global” + “For all” after the update (nothing changes); projects you create or import from now on start locked (Off).
- Private to-dos and tickets (#386): cards, tags (all cards with the tag) and tickets have a “Private” switch. Agents never see private items – not in lists, counts, searches (also JQL), links or calendar sources; direct access returns `not_found`. Only you set or remove “Private”; a private card converted to a ticket stays private. New option “New cards private” in Settings (default off). Cards get an optional project (picker in the card dialog, default: the active project).
- System notes (#386): cards, tickets and comments remember who created them (you, an agent with its session or model name, or Paneldeck); system notes show a badge, the to-do list has a “System notes” show/hide filter and the search knows `systemNote` and `private`. Project backups keep the private flag and the origin of tickets and comments (older versions ignore these fields).
- New agent switches in Settings → Agents, both off by default (#386): “Agents may create projects” (ticket projects only, linked to the agent's project, marked “created by an agent”) and “Agents may delete tickets and their own comments” – deleted items go to the new trash (Settings → Agents → Trash) where only you restore them or delete them permanently.
- MCP (#386): new error code `project_locked` (the target project is locked for this agent; never names the project), `create_session` parameter `project`, `whoami` field `access` (reachable projects with level), `list_projects` fields `agent_level` and `created_by_agent`, `origin` on tickets, comments and to-dos, `project` on to-dos, `ignored_tags` on `add_todo`, and the tools `create_project`, `delete_ticket`, `update_comment` (only comments written by agents) and `delete_comment`. `mcp-log.jsonl` entries get a `project` field. A one-time notice per agent and project offers “Release …” when an agent hits a locked project. Restoring a backup from before 3.2.0 shows a notice that agent access of ticket projects is at the defaults.
- Chats with local models use the lower of the model's level and their project's level (#386); a chat in a locked project gets no Paneldeck tools, its own file and command tools stay. For chats the four new tools are off by default and checked per model under Settings → Models (#393); once checked, chats get `create_project`, `delete_ticket` and `delete_comment` only while the matching switch is on (`update_comment` needs Control only, like for external agents); like every write they need your approval and plan mode blocks them.
- **Own background for themes:** a theme can now have a background behind the whole window – an **image** (PNG, JPEG, WebP or BMP; animated files show their first frame), a **gradient** (linear with an angle or radial with centre and radius, 2–4 freely placed stops, colours also as theme links such as `@accent/40`, light dithering against banding), a **colour** or a **pattern** (dots, lines, grid, checks, diagonal lines and noise drawn in the theme's colours, or your own tile image; size 25–400 % and offset). Images are aligned to one of 9 anchor points with a margin per side and scaled with *Fill* (default), *Fit*, *Stretch* or *Original*; plus opacity, brightness (−100 … +100 %), blur (0–40 pt) and a colour overlay. Set it up in the theme editor under the new *Background* tab with a live preview, or copy it with *Take background from …* (#387)
- Background **areas**: terminals, editor, to-do/planning, chat and sidebar (title bar and tabs follow the sidebar) each let the background show through with their own opacity (multiplied with the window opacity) or stay opaque; dialogs, menus, tooltips and input fields always stay opaque. In terminals only the default background is see-through, and the minimum text contrast is computed against the surface over the image (#387)
- The background is one continuous picture across the window; every window renders it for its own size, computed once in the background and cached (after resizing it is recomputed after a short pause, no extra redraws) (#387)
- New design check rule **Background** in the theme editor: text per area against the image (average and brightest/darkest 10 %), with a suggestion for brightness or overlay opacity (#387)
- Images are copied (at most 3840 px) into a folder next to the theme file, `themes/<file>/`; the original stays untouched. Renaming, duplicating and deleting a theme take the folder along, and *Clean up → Custom themes* removes the images too. Themes with images are exported and imported as `.zip` (`theme.json` + images); import checks names (no paths), sizes and that every image can be read (#387)
- New switch **Reduce transparency** under *Settings → Appearance* (off by default): all surfaces opaque, no theme background. New optional `settings.json` field `reduce_transparency` (#387)
- New optional object `background` in `themes/*.json`; invalid values fall back to their default and the theme stays valid, unknown values are kept. Agent interface: `theme_get` returns the new read-only field `background` (settings as in the file plus `mean_brightness`; the image only as a bare file name, never image data) (#387)
- **Editor: horizontal scrollbar:** with word wrap off, every split shows a scrollbar at the bottom of the text when lines are wider than the view (hidden otherwise, styled like the vertical one). Drag the thumb or click beside it to page left/right; Shift+wheel, the wheel over the scrollbar, the touchpad and cursor-follow keep working. Scrolling now stops a little after the end of the longest line instead of going on into empty space; tabs and wide characters count as on screen, and in large files the longest line is measured in the background (#389).
- Paths follow the path kind of the target shell everywhere (#392): the folder fields of terminal templates, profiles (*Start folder*), the process list and the planning work folder are now the same field as in *New session* – for WSL with the *Windows path / WSL path* switch, completion, check and recently used folders, for Git Bash also `/c/x` and `~`, for every shell `~` and `~/x`; what a typed path stands for is shown small underneath.
- Ctrl+click on a Linux path in the terminal output (`/home/me/x.rs:12`, `~/notes.md`, `/mnt/c/…`, Git Bash `/c/…`) opens text files in the Paneldeck editor at that line and folders in Explorer; file links set by programs (OSC 8) from a WSL session point to the file in that distribution.
- The editor opens and saves files in WSL distributions (`\\wsl.localhost\…`, loaded in the background) and offers *Copy as Linux path* for them; agents stay limited to local Windows files.
- `create_session` (MCP) accepts Git Bash paths (`/c/x`, `/cygdrive/c/x`) and `~` as `cwd` for Git Bash sessions, `~` for other Windows shells and `~/x` for WSL shells (checked in WSL like an absolute path).
- WSL: the drive mount point of a distribution (`[automount] root` in `/etc/wsl.conf`) is respected when converting `C:\x` ↔ `/mnt/c/x`.
- New tutorial step *Paths for every shell* (chapter *More tools*, “What's new” in 3.2.0).
- Chats with local models: in a small context (e.g. 8192 tokens) the compact chat instructions now list only a core of tools (sessions and terminals, commands, files, the chat's to-dos and questions, the simple to-do list) plus the new chat tool `more_tools`, and the other tools are loaded on demand: without arguments it lists the other tools the model may use right now, with `name` it returns one tool's purpose, fields, approval and an example; the model then calls that tool by its name, and it stays listed in the chat's next requests. Every tool the model's level, mode, switches and tool selection allow is reachable this way. Large contexts still get every tool directly. Level, switches, approvals and plan mode apply unchanged; `more_tools` only reads and never describes a tool the model may not use (#393)
- Chats with local models can use the new tools of 3.2.0: the Time tab and the calendar (alarms, timers, stopwatches, clocks, birthdays, calendar events), the editor's formatter (`format_file`, with `validate` without a switch and the other ops only with *Agents may format*, as for external agents), diff (`editor_diff`) and list of actions on save (`list_save_actions`), and creating projects, deleting tickets and comments and changing comments (`create_project`, `delete_ticket`, `update_comment`, `delete_comment`). They are off by default for chats and can be switched on per model: the tool list under Settings → Models shows them unchecked and marked "(off by default)". Once checked they follow the model's level, the switches and the access per project like for external agents, and changes need your approval. New optional field `enabled_tools` per model in `settings.json`; older files load unchanged (#393)

### Changed

- The database gets schema versions 11 and 12 (tables of the Time tab and of the calendar); older Paneldeck versions refuse the upgraded database, a backup is made before the migration (#164).
- Process list: ids of deleted entries are no longer given to new entries (new optional field `next_id` in `processes.json`) (#383).
- Editor: *Show difference* in the info bar of a file changed on disk opens the new comparison tab instead of a separate window (#385)
- `tools/list` shows the tools of the highest level an agent can reach anywhere; every call is then checked against the project it touches (#386). `screenshot` of the whole window only works while no project is locked for the agent and every Paneldeck window shows the Terminal Hub without an open dialog, menu or notice and without a chip of a ticket the agent may not see (otherwise `forbidden` – use `session`); `screenshot` with `session` shows only that session's content, without its header line.
- Approval prompts no longer invite approving without looking (#390): the "Waiting for you" card in the sidebar now has **Open** first, in the accent colour, followed by **Yes** and **No** without colour (hover texts unchanged: Enter / Esc). No button has the keyboard focus by itself, Tab reaches *Open* first, and Yes/No only work half a second after the prompt appeared. In chats, **Allow** and the plan card's **Execute** no longer have a colour fill either.
- The "Waiting for you" card shows what is requested (#390): the tool (Bash, Edit, …) and the command or path from Claude's request, marked *from the session* as untrusted text and never read as markup. Padding is collapsed and line breaks show as ⏎; a long request shows its beginning and end with the number of hidden characters, the full text is in the tooltip. Yes/No are only offered when exactly one call matches, its text stands in the approval dialog on the terminal screen and it fits completely into the card – and are checked against the screen again when clicked. Otherwise the card shows the uncertainty (several possible calls side by side, *probably – not seen in the terminal yet*, a shortened request or Claude's message) with **Open** only. Chats waiting for an approval now appear in the card too, with the tool, the target and all arguments (*from the model*); Yes/No decide exactly that call. The request is only kept in memory, never in `state.json`.
- MCP: `cwd` of WSL sessions (`list_sessions`, `whoami`, `create_session`) is now reported as `\\wsl.localhost\<distro>\…` (before `\\wsl$\…`), and `/mnt/c/x` as `C:\x`.
- Claude hooks for WSL sessions use one settings file per distribution (`claude-hooks-wsl-<distro>.json`); the program path is quoted for the shell.
- A start folder `~/x` of a WSL session is resolved with the distribution's home instead of starting in `~`.
- Chats with local models: the compact chat instructions for small contexts are much smaller and leave at least half of the room for the conversation at every level and mode, however many tools are switched on; plan mode now fits into 8K with all tools (#393)
- Chats with local models at level Control without expert mode: the automatic context of an Ollama model stays at 32768 tokens; when the full chat instructions do not fit there, the chat uses the compact version and loads other tools with `more_tools` (#393)

### Fixed

- Terminals: a profile's environment variables could overwrite Paneldeck's own variables such as `PANELDECK_SESSION` and `PANELDECK_SESSION_KEY`; `PANELDECK_*` and internal names are now never taken from profiles, templates or sessions, and Paneldeck's values are set last (#384)
- Process list: environment variables of a process with a WSL shell did not reach the Linux process; they are now passed through `WSLENV` (#384)
- Drag & drop of WSL files and *Insert path* in the file tree of a WSL session inserted `//wsl.localhost/…` instead of the Linux path; a file of another WSL distribution is no longer inserted (a note says why). Claude option paths typed as `\\wsl.localhost\…` are converted to Linux paths as well.
- The working folder reported by a WSL session became `\\wsl$\…` (without the distribution for `wsl.exe` without `-d`, without the WSL character mapping) and `/mnt/c/…` stayed a WSL path; it is now `\\wsl.localhost\<distro>\…` or `C:\…`.
- The process list rejected Linux folders for WSL shells; they now start via `wsl.exe --cd`.
- The start folder of terminal templates ignored the template's shell.
- *New session here* in the file tree of a WSL folder did not use the WSL shell of that distribution.
- `paneldeck --open` and `paneldeck --edit` with an absolute Linux path resolved it as a Windows path; they now use the distribution of the session the command was typed in (otherwise the default distribution).
- A chat created from a WSL session with a Linux folder had no file tools; its folder is now `\\wsl.localhost\<distro>\…` (secret folders in the Linux home stay blocked).
- cmd.exe sessions with a network or WSL start folder silently started in `C:\Windows`; they now start in the home folder with a note.

### Security

- Agents can no longer open, edit or save files in Paneldeck's data folder (rules, settings, process list …) through the editor tools (#383).
- Git: every git call of Paneldeck (status and changes in the Terminal Hub, the git tools of chats, comparison and change markers) now runs hardened – no network at all (all protocols off, no lazy fetch of a partial clone, no credential helper, no prompts), no fsmonitor, and for reading commands no hooks and no signature check – so a crafted `.git/config` (e.g. from an unpacked archive) cannot start programs through `ext::` remotes, `gpg.program` or hooks when Paneldeck reads a repository; git calls of the comparison stop after 10 s (#385)
- Agents only reach the sessions, processes, to-dos, tickets and calendar entries of projects released for them (#386); new projects are locked by default.
- Hidden items never leak through counts, sprint/version totals, maintenance findings, ticket-field values, `parent` queries, the search schema, `search_history`, session ids or error lists; sessions created by external agents count as external; `paneldeck --open` from a session opens in that session's project; sidebar project ids are never reused and unknown project ids are locked; waiting calls are checked again when they finish (#386).
- Agent calls without a project (General) are checked with the level of the call: `format_file` with an `op` other than `validate` needs Control in General too, also for chats; a tool that is not classified otherwise counts as General (#386).
- Chat requests only offer and document the tools the chat's project allows and name the effective level; a chat created by an external agent counts as external (#386).
- The editor tools and `list_save_actions` leave out documents, files and rules in the folders of locked projects (`project_locked` for direct access); `get_ticket` names only visible sessions in `linked_terminals` and shows comment history without comment text; `list_timers`/`list_stopwatches` return `ticket` = `null` for tickets the agent may not see (#386).
- Chat file tools in a WSL folder cannot reach Windows secrets or Paneldeck's data folder through the drive mounts (`/mnt/c/…`); chat folders that contain a drive mount (`/`, `/mnt`, `/mnt/c`) are refused, links inside WSL folders are not followed, and `.credentials.json`, `.claude.json` and `.git-credentials` are blocked everywhere.
- `\\wsl$` and `\\wsl.localhost` only count as local paths when WSL is installed; OSC 8 links to `file://wsl.localhost/…` with `.`/`..` segments are refused; paths that would type control characters into a terminal are not inserted.
- Chat file tools in WSL folders wait (`busy`) until the distribution's drive mount and home are known, re-check every path inside WSL, never follow links and block the home WSL reports (#392).
